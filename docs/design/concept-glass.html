Glass, color and one question

The same simple structure, now in a glass design language: frosted panels that let the page and a soft color field show through, bright highlight edges, capsule buttons, and a color for every kind of rule so you can tell them apart at a glance.

How rules work

shown at the top of Settings

Every download is checked in this order. The first match decides the folder; if nothing matches, it goes to Everything else.
